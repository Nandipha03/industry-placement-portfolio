Welcome to my professional portfolio.

I am a third-year Diploma in ICT Applications Development student at Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT), currently gaining workplace experience at the Judicial Inspectorate of Correctional Services (JICS).

This portfolio documents my professional journey, the work I have been involved in, and the knowledge and skills I am developing through my industry placement.

It includes project-related activities, Agile deliverables, evidence of my work, and reflections on my learning and growth.

My goal is to connect what I have learned through my studies with real workplace challenges and continue developing my skills in information technology.
