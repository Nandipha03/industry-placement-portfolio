Agile Deliverable 05

Agile Logbook

This logbook records key project activities, progress, challenges and learning during the development of the proposed ICCV digital platform at the Judicial Inspectorate for Correctional Services (JICS). It reflects the project's current stage and identifies the next steps as more information becomes available.

Project ICCV Digital Platform Management
Organisation Judicial Inspectorate for Correctional Services (JICS)

1. Purpose of the Logbook

An Agile Logbook provides a record of project activities, decisions, progress, challenges and lessons learned. It helps the team keep track of what has happened, understand obstacles and identify actions that may be needed next.

This logbook documents the work undertaken so far, including the preparation of the ICCV survey and the development of project planning deliverables. It also records activities that remain dependent on survey distribution, responses and further team discussions.

Record-keeping note: The entries below describe known project activities and current progress. Exact dates have not been included where they have not been confirmed. Future activities are identified as planned rather than completed.

3. Current Challenges and Lessons Learned

Access to intended users

One of the main challenges is ensuring that ICCVs can receive and access the survey. Organisational email and SIM-card-related difficulties may affect participation. This has highlighted the importance of considering accessibility when planning project activities.

Using evidence to guide priorities

Although the team has identified potential operational challenges, the survey responses are needed to understand how frequently particular issues occur and how users experience them. Recording and analysing this information will help the team make more informed decisions about which problems to address first.

Collaboration and documentation

Reviewing and restructuring the survey as a team demonstrated the value of discussing work, correcting weaknesses and improving deliverables together. Documenting activities and decisions can also help the team maintain a shared understanding of progress and explain the reasons behind future priorities.

4. Planned Next Steps

Moving the project forward

  • Investigate and address the barriers affecting survey distribution.
  • Distribute the prepared survey and collect responses from participating ICCVs.
  • Review the responses collaboratively and identify recurring challenges and relevant statistics.
  • Document the findings in a report that can support discussions with project stakeholders.
  • Review the user stories and proposed backlog priorities against the evidence collected.
  • Continue documenting project activities, decisions, obstacles and learning as the work progresses.

Learning and Development

Working on this project has helped me understand the importance of planning, collaboration and documenting progress throughout a project. Preparing the survey showed me that requirements need to reflect the actual work and experiences of intended users. Reviewing the questions as a team also highlighted the value of feedback and making improvements before moving forward.

I have also learned that identifying a possible problem is only the beginning. The team needs reliable information to understand the extent of the problem and decide which areas should receive priority. This makes documentation and evidence-based decision-making important parts of the process.

Although the project has not followed a confirmed formal sprint schedule, preparing the Agile deliverables has helped me understand how work can be organised into manageable tasks and refined as new information becomes available. Going forward, I want to improve how I record project activities and reflect on what each stage teaches me about working in an IT team.
