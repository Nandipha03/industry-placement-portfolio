Professional Practice 3 · Industry Placement

Overall Reflection

My industry placement at the Judicial Inspectorate for Correctional Services (JICS) has helped me connect what I have learned through my ICT studies with the realities of a professional working environment. Through the ICCV job management platform project, I have gained a better understanding of requirements analysis, Agile planning, communication and the importance of considering users when exploring digital solutions.

My Experience and Professional Growth

My placement has given me an opportunity to explore how information technology can support everyday work processes. The ICCV job management platform project has helped me look beyond the technical side of a system and consider the people who would use it, the information they need and the challenges they experience.

Working on this project has also shown me that understanding a problem should come before proposing a solution. Before deciding what a digital platform should include, it is important to understand the current process, identify areas that could be improved and consider the needs of the intended users.

This experience has encouraged me to think more carefully about how technology can improve efficiency, reduce unnecessary repetition and support better information management.

Skills and Knowledge I Have Developed

Throughout this project, I have developed a better understanding of several skills and concepts that are important in IT project work:

  • Requirements analysis: Understanding users, their responsibilities and their challenges before deciding what a system should do.
  • Agile planning: Organising project work into user stories, prioritised tasks, backlogs and manageable activities.
  • Communication and collaboration: Recognising the importance of clarifying expectations, asking questions and coordinating with others.
  • Problem-solving and critical thinking: Examining existing processes and considering practical limitations before proposing possible digital improvements.

I have also gained a clearer understanding of how project documentation helps organise ideas, communicate decisions and keep project activities focused on the intended objectives.

Challenges and How I Responded

One of the challenges during the placement has been working through project activities while balancing my workplace responsibilities and academic commitments. Progress can depend on access to information, feedback and clarification from the relevant people, which means that some activities cannot always move forward as quickly as expected.

These challenges have taught me the importance of patience, planning and keeping track of outstanding tasks. They have also shown me why communication is essential when working on a project, particularly when requirements need to be clarified before the next steps can be completed.

Rather than assuming that every challenge can be solved immediately, I have learned to identify what can be progressed independently and what requires further information or feedback. This has helped me approach the work more realistically.

Evidence of My Development

The following project deliverables provide evidence of my learning and show how I have been applying Agile concepts and project planning practices throughout the placement.

  • Agile Team Charter: Demonstrates my understanding of project purpose, responsibilities and working agreements.
  • User Stories & Tasks: Shows how user needs can be expressed as stories with associated tasks and acceptance criteria.
  • Product Backlog: Reflects my learning about organising and prioritising proposed features and project work.
  • Sprint Backlog: Demonstrates how selected work can be grouped around a sprint goal and tracked.
  • Agile Logbook: Records project progress, decisions, challenges and learning throughout the placement.

What I Would Approach Differently Next Time

If I were to approach a similar project again, I would place even greater emphasis on confirming the project requirements and expectations at the beginning. Having a clear understanding of the scope, responsibilities, available information and expected deliverables would help me plan my work more effectively.

I would also aim to identify dependencies and possible delays earlier, maintain a consistent record of decisions and questions, and allow sufficient time for feedback and revisions. These steps would help me manage my workload while keeping the project documentation organised and accurate.

An Experience I Will Carry Forward

Overall, my placement has helped me understand that working in IT involves more than developing or selecting technology. It also requires understanding people, analysing problems, communicating clearly and making decisions based on real needs and practical circumstances.

As I continue developing professionally, I want to strengthen my analytical and problem-solving abilities and become more confident in contributing to projects. I will carry these lessons forward into my future studies and career, particularly in areas involving business analysis, systems analysis and IT project management.
